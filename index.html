The officially numbered Sales Invoices declared to BIR — this feeds the VAT summary in Reports and the Income Statement. Rows sync in automatically from Daily Sales whenever a BIR receipt no. is entered there, so this register only ever holds transactions that actually have a BIR receipt no. — there's no manual "log an invoice" form anymore. Older entries carried over from the Declarations sheet stay here as history (and can still be deleted if a cleanup is needed). Net sales/VAT are computed by entity — LIC Printing Shop (Sole Proprietorship) is VAT-registered (Net = Gross ÷ 1.12); LIC Printing Corporation is Non-VAT, so Gross = Net and VAT shows ₱0.
